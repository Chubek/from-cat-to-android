From C/A/T to Android

You are writing a book, in LaTeX, called "From C/A/T to Android". This book is:

1. A history of Unix, from its birth at the Unix Labs via its connection to TROFF, to BSD, to Unix Wars, to Linux (and GNU) and other Unix-like OS's;
2. How Unix works (mostly based on Lion's commentary), xv6, etc;
3. History of Unix tools, and their evolution;
4. History of graphics in Unix, from CURSES library, to bitmap terminals, to X Windowing system, to Wayland;
5. The history of Linux and open source;
6. Art of Unix Programming (based on Eric S. Raymond's book);
7. The current Unix landescape, Linux, Android, gaming on Linux, etc;
8. Historical use of Unix in Workstations


Add more subjects as you desire. Chapters must be long, and expansive. Mind the build system (`build.lua`, `Makefile`, `.latexmkrc`).

`from-cat-to-android.ltx` is the index. Chapters are in `chapters`.

Use the books in `sources` as a source of truth.
